## Superlogical

[Website](https://www.superlogical.com/)

A terminal multiplexer built around long-lived sessions that can be resumed
from another device and shared live, with native scrollback and selection.
Could be the multiplexer that finally earns a place here: I never found
multiplexers very useful, as they tend to do too many things (tmux, for
example).

_Status (2026-10-22): not released yet, beta waitlist only. Announced clients
are web and macOS/iOS, so check Linux support before trying it._
